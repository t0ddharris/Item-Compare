Item Compare — v1.2.1

Compare inventory, shop, and storage gear with your equipped items at a glance.
For The Blood of Dawnwalker 1.1 on PC.

FEATURES
Shows equipped gear in stock tooltips to the right of the hovered inventory
item. Both equipped rings are stacked vertically, centered against the
hovered tooltip. Empty slots are omitted. Items without equipment slots,
including consumables and materials, keep a single tooltip.

Hovering merchant stock or stored gear shows equipped gear to the left of
the hovered tooltip with the same EQUIPPED heading. Your character's items
keep a single tooltip in shops and storage.

The EQUIPPED heading follows the game's text language in Simplified Chinese,
Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Japanese, Polish, Korean, and Russian.
Other languages use English.

Already-equipped items show only their own tooltip. The original tooltip
keeps the game's stat differences and ring comparison control.

Use Left Shift + mouse wheel or the controller's right stick to scroll the
hovered item and its visible equipped comparisons together.

REQUIREMENTS
The Blood of Dawnwalker 1.1 on PC. No additional mods or UE4SS are required.
Manual installation is supported; mod-manager deployment has not been tested.

INSTALL / UPGRADE
Close the game. Copy all three files together into:
<game folder>\Dawnwalker\Content\Paks\~mods

00000000_ItemCompare_P.pak
00000000_ItemCompare_P.ucas
00000000_ItemCompare_P.utoc

Create ~mods if needed. Replace all three Item Compare files together.
Leave Better Quickslots and its files unchanged.

UNINSTALL
Close the game and remove only the three files listed above.
Other mods and UE4SS can stay installed.
